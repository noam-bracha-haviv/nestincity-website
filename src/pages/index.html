The crisis

They are
vanishing.

Wild bee populations are in dramatic decline and the city is a large part of the problem. But it can also be a large part of the solution.

  • A single small urban garden can sustain a whole population for years.
  • More than 1,250 species of wild bees are documented in Israel.
  • A single visit by a wild bee equals dozens of honeybee visits.

In the past twenty years, up to 40% of wild bee populations have
disappeared.
The causes accumulate open land turned to concrete, pesticides, climate
change and above
all, the disappearance of nesting habitats.
A nesting habitat in a community garden is an
active extension
of nature into the place where it is most missing.

Wild bees

22,000+ species · 1,100 in Israel alone

  • Most are solitary no hive, no queen, no honey.
  • Pollinators many times more efficient than the honeybee.
  • Almost never sting. Too busy working.
  • In decline under threat

The honeybee

Apis mellifera · one familiar species

  • Most are solitary no hive, no queen, no honey.
  • Pollinators many times more efficient than the honeybee.
  • Almost never sting. Too busy working.
  • In decline under threat

They've been working for us for millions of years. Now is the time to stop, to know them and to build them a home.
